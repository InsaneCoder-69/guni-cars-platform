GUNI CARS Platform Blueprint 1. Executive Summary & Problem Analysis
1. Executive Summary & Strategic Context
Addressing institutional data fragmentation, awareness gaps, and ranking documentation at Ganpat University

The Current Problem

  • Decentralized Data Silos: Research grants, consultancy contracts, and student innovations remain isolated across individual laptops, lab notebooks, or departmental files across 12+ institutes.
  • Severe Ranking Audit Penalty: Every year, university coordinators manually chase faculty for sanction letters, utilization certificates (UC), and audited accounts. Missing documentation leads to underreported research metrics in NIRF and NAAC filings.
  • Zero Campus-Wide Awareness: Students, research scholars, and junior faculty are unaware of ongoing funded projects, open research openings, and high-end CoE facilities on campus.
  • No Real-time Revenue Tracking: Constituent institutes lack a live tracking panel to benchmark performance against the university's ₹4.25 Cr annual target.

The Platform Solution

  • Unified University Repository: A single authoritative database recording all sponsored research, contract R&D, student projects, and IP commercialization.
  • 1-Click Ranking Compliance Engine: Automated export of pre-formatted NIRF Data Capture System (DCS) and NAAC Criterion 3 data sheets with verified document links.
  • Public Showcase & Discovery Portal: Public-facing searchable catalog of university research projects, patents, and CoE facilities (3D printers, 5G lab) to attract corporate tie-ups.
  • Live Executive CARS Dashboard: Real-time progress bars tracking institute achievement against designated revenue quotas.

GUNI CARS Platform Blueprint 5. Rankings Compliance & Implementation
8. Direct University Ranking & Filing Mechanics
Automating annual audit submissions from continuous real-time data collection

NIRF (Research & Professional Practice - RPC)

  • FSR (Funded Sponsored Research): Generates verified tables of government and industry projects with audited sanctioned amounts.
  • FCP (Funded Consultancy Projects): Auto-calculates exact client consultancy cash receipts per financial year with downloadable proof attachments.

NAAC (Criterion 3: Research & Innovations)

  • Metric 3.2.1 / 3.2.2: Extramural research funding data pre-formatted into official NAAC Excel templates.
  • Metric 3.4.3 / 3.4.4: Complete patent registry (published and granted).
  • Metric 3.5.1 / 3.5.2: Audited consultancy revenue and corporate training fees.

QS & Times Higher Education (THE)

  • Industry Income / Knowledge Transfer: Instant calculation of research revenue generated from industry per academic staff.

ARIIA (Innovation Ranking)

  • Tracks progression of Technology Readiness Levels (TRLs), working prototypes, student startups, and IP commercialization royalties.
