1. Background

Research consultancy has emerged as a key driver of innovation, technology development, industry collaboration, and institutional revenue generation in higher education. Unlike routine consultancy services, research consultancy focuses on solving complex industrial, societal, and technological challenges through sponsored research, product development, prototype validation, technology transfer, intellectual property (IP) generation, and commercialization.

GANPAT University has established strong multidisciplinary research capabilities through its Schools, Institutes, Centres of Excellence, advanced laboratories, pilot manufacturing facilities, and industry partnerships. However, there is currently no centralized mechanism to systematically monitor, evaluate, and enhance institute-wise research consultancy revenue.

This proposal aims to establish an Institute-wise Research Consultancy Revenue Monitoring and Enhancement System under the Centre for Advanced Research Studies (CARS) to strengthen research collaborations, improve institutional performance, and increase externally funded research revenue.

The initiative aligns with the objectives of NEP 2020, National Research Foundation (ANRF), Atmanirbhar Bharat, Viksit Bharat 2047, and national priorities promoting research excellence, innovation, entrepreneurship, and industry-academia collaboration.

2. Objectives

The major objectives are:

Establish a centralized database of research consultancy projects across all institutes.

Monitor institute-wise research consultancy revenue from sponsored research, contract R&D, technology transfer, and innovation projects.

Promote multidisciplinary industry-academia collaborative research.

Increase externally funded research projects from government agencies, industries, and international organizations.

Encourage patent generation, technology transfer, licensing, and commercialization.

Develop institute-wise research consultancy revenue targets and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).

Support accreditation, NIRF, NAAC, QS, THE, and research ranking submissions through robust research performance data.

Facilitate evidence-based policy decisions to strengthen the University's research and innovation ecosystem.
